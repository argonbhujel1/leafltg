The Story of Leaf Letang

Leaf Letang Enterprises is rooted in Letang, Morang, where locally available betel-nut leaves become the starting point for sustainable products and economic opportunity.

Founded by local entrepreneurs, the enterprise began production in November 2021 with a focus on transforming fallen leaves into duna and tapari.

The business connects farmers, local resources, manufacturing and wider markets — creating value from something that would otherwise have limited economic use.

Reported initial joint investment was approximately NPR 2.1 million. Historical production has been reported at approximately 10,000 pieces per day (this figure is historical and not presented as current capacity).

Local Resource Utilization

The main raw material is fallen betel-nut (areca-nut) leaves. By creating demand for these leaves, the enterprise supports a local collection network and manufacturing activity in Letang Municipality.

From manufacturing to market expansion, the focus remains on practical products — duna and tapari — that serve everyday and traditional food-service needs while keeping value close to the community that supplies the leaves.
