Community

GROWING WITH OUR COMMUNITY.

Farmers, workers, and local entrepreneurship around Leaf Letang Enterprises in Letang Municipality.

Farmers

By creating demand for fallen betel-nut leaves, the enterprise offers a path for agricultural by-products to generate additional value for farmers and collectors in the region.

Employment

Manufacturing, preparation, packaging and related activities create work opportunities rooted in Letang — connecting local resources to practical products.

Local Entrepreneurship

Leaf Letang Enterprises was founded by seven local entrepreneurs who saw potential in turning fallen leaves into useful food-service products and community opportunity.

Municipality

Letang Municipality has expressed support for local entrepreneurship and sustainable industries. The enterprise operates within this local context — manufacturing in Letang and drawing on resources from the surrounding agricultural landscape.

Value Flow

Farmers → Leaf Collection → Manufacturing → Employment → Markets → Community
