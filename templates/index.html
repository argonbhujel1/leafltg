How We Work

FROM FALLEN LEAF
TO FINISHED PRODUCT.

01

Collection

Fallen betel-nut leaves are gathered from local agricultural areas around Letang.

02

Selection & Preparation

Leaves are sorted and prepared for forming into food-service products.

03

Machine Forming

Prepared leaves are shaped into duna and tapari using production equipment.

04

Quality Check

Each batch is reviewed for consistency and suitability for food service use.

05

Packaging

Finished products are packaged for local distribution and bulk supply.

06

Distribution

Products reach markets in Letang and beyond, connecting local value to wider demand.
