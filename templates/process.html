Process

FROM FALLEN LEAF
TO FINISHED PRODUCT.

A clear production path — from collection of betel-nut leaves to packaged duna and tapari.

01

Collection

Fallen betel-nut leaves are collected from agricultural areas around Letang. Creating demand for this by-product supports local collection activity.

02

Sorting

Leaves are sorted for suitability. Only leaves appropriate for forming into food-service products proceed to the next stage.

03

Preparation

Selected leaves are prepared for machine forming, ensuring they are ready for consistent shaping into duna and tapari.

04

Forming

Prepared leaves are shaped using production equipment into traditional leaf bowls (duna) and plates (tapari).

05

Quality Checking

Finished pieces are checked for consistency and suitability for food-service applications.

06

Packaging

Products are packaged for distribution — ready for local markets, events, and bulk business orders.

07

Distribution

Packaged products reach customers and partners, carrying value from Letang’s leaves to wider markets.
