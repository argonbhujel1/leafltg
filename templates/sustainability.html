Sustainability

TURNING AN AGRICULTURAL
BY-PRODUCT INTO OPPORTUNITY.

Fallen betel-nut leaves become the foundation for products, employment, and local economic value.

Betel-nut (areca-nut) cultivation leaves behind large volumes of fallen leaves. Leaf Letang Enterprises works with this agricultural by-product — collecting, preparing and forming leaves into biodegradable duna and tapari.

The approach is practical: use what nature already provides, create demand for a local resource, support manufacturing employment, and offer leaf-based alternatives for food-service applications.

A Circular Path of Value

Leaves → Farmers → Factory → Products → Markets → Community Value
🍃

Fallen Leaves

Using betel-nut leaves that would otherwise have limited economic use.

👨‍🌾

Farmers & Collection

Supporting local collection of leaves as a supplementary activity around agriculture.

🏭

Manufacturing

Local production of duna and tapari in Letang Municipality.

📦

Biodegradable Products

Leaf-based food-service items as practical alternatives in traditional and everyday use.

👷

Employment

Creating work in collection, preparation, forming, packaging and related activities.

🏘️

Local Economic Value

Keeping more of the value chain — from leaf to product — close to the community.
